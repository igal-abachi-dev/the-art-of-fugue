### Decision (what the site uses)

- **Used (18 pieces):** I-XI, XII (rectus), XIII inversus (3 voices), XIV, Canon 1-4.
- **Derived pieces (2 pieces):** XII inversus and XIII rectus, which are in the PDF but not in the MXL.
  Built as mirrors of their partner pieces (rhythm, voices, onsets), then every pitch is replaced by
  the notehead engraved in the PDF (`tools/artfugue/pdfnotes.py`). Result: all 56 and 72 bars agree
  with the engraving (`verified: true`, see `pdf-audit.md`). Owner decision (T1.3.4): patched
  bar by bar against the PDF.
- **Excluded (4):** `x1`, `x2`, `x3` (appendix early versions) and `x4` (4-voice XIII).
  They stay in the data (`status: excluded`) for reference and are not shown.
- **Repeats:** only Canon 1 (bars 5-80) and Canon 3 (bars 9-75) carry repeat marks among the used
  pieces. Both written and unfolded note files exist. Whether the pianist takes the repeats is
  decided after alignment (T2.3.3); `repeats_taken` is `null` until then.
