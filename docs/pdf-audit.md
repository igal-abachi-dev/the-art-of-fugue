# Score vs engraving: pitch audit (T1.1.2, T1.3.4)

Generated by `tools/make_docs.py` from `data/work/score/`. The PDF (Contrapunctus Press) is the source
of truth. It was engraved with LilyPond, so every notehead, accidental and clef is a glyph of the
font `Emmentaler-20` in the text layer. `tools/artfugue/pdfnotes.py` reads them (pitch from the
glyph's height over the staff lines, the clef, the key signature and the accidentals of the bar;
ledger lines decide which staff a note between the staves belongs to).

## Method

- **Reader.** Noteheads of every system, with bar, x position and pitch. Checked against the MXL
  on all pieces: it reproduces the MXL's pitches in the large majority of bars. Of 5 disagreements
  inspected on the page image, 4 were wrong notes in the MXL and 1 was a reader bug (a clef change
  in mid-bar), which was fixed; the other disagreements were not inspected one by one.
- **Audit (`audit_pdf.py`).** For every bar the notes that start in it are paired with the
  noteheads (best pairing by pitch, then horizontal position). A bar passes when every note pairs
  with a notehead of the same pitch. Tied continuations (extra head in the first column without the
  accidental) and unisons shared by two voices are allowed for.
- **Correction.** A bar that fails is changed only if the changed bar then passes completely and no
  pitch moves by more than 3 semitones. Everything changed is listed below
  (`data/work/score/pdf_corrections.json`). Rhythm, onsets and voices are never changed.
- **Limits.** Only pitch is checked, not duration, and a wrong note that lies next to a tied
  note, or several wrong notes in one bar, can survive (a test with artificial errors restores about
  80-90 % of isolated errors). Bars listed under "still differing" were not changed.
